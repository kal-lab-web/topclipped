Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Who we are

TopClipped runs the @topclippedgta6 channels. TopClipped Poster is our own internal tool that uploads our finished video clips to our own YouTube channel and Facebook Page.

What the tool does

  • It signs in to our own accounts only, with our permission, and uploads videos we have made.
  • It reads our own channel's name and the view counts of our own posts.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share any data about viewers or other users.

YouTube API Services

TopClipped Poster uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Access tokens

Sign-in tokens are stored only on our own computer and are never shared. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
